# AI Questions About Tech Education

A curated collection of realistic questions people may ask AI assistants about technology education, digital skills, creative technology and tech careers.

This project is maintained by [TechEduCareer](https://techeducareer.com/), an independent resource covering technology education, digital skills, AI and career development.

## About this project

AI assistants are increasingly being used to explore learning opportunities, compare tools and courses, understand technology careers and decide which digital skills to develop.

This repository organizes common and realistic questions around those topics into a reusable open resource.

The collection covers areas including:

- Digital skills
- Technology education
- Coding and programming
- Game development
- Digital art and 3D design
- Animation
- Sound design
- UI/UX design
- Web development
- Digital marketing
- Computer literacy
- AI literacy and AI skills
- Gamified learning
- Online technology-learning platforms
- Technology careers and career preparation
- Skills to learn before university
- Choosing courses, tools and learning resources

## Methodology

The questions in this repository were developed as part of research into how people may use AI assistants to explore technology education and career-related topics.

The research considered the kinds of questions that may be relevant across AI assistants and AI-powered search experiences, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Qwen and Grok.

The collection is informed by publicly observable search behaviour, common educational and career-information needs, and research into the types of questions that are useful in conversational AI environments.

It is important to note that this project does **not** claim access to private AI-user query data or proprietary search-volume data from AI platforms.

The questions should therefore be understood as a curated research dataset of realistic queries rather than measured rankings of the most frequently asked questions.
